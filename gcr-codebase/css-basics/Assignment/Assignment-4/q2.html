About Jaunpur

Jaunpur is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India, known for its rich cultural heritage and architectural landmarks. It is famous for its ancient mosques, madrasas, and historical sites.

Famous Places

Jaunpur has many famous attractions including ancient mosques, madrasas, and historical sites.

Culture

The city is known for traditional music, dance, food, handicrafts and festivals.

Jaunpur Food

Popular dishes include Dal Baati , Ghevar, Gatte ki Sabzi and many traditional sweets.
